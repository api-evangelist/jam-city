# Jam City

> Jam City is a Culver City, California mobile entertainment company founded in 2010 that develops and publishes free-to-play mobile games — the Cookie Jam and Panda Pop franchises plus licensed titles such as Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery, Disney Emoji Blitz and Family Guy: The Quest for Stuff. It reports over $4 billion in aggregate lifetime bookings and nearly 1.5 billion downloads. Jam City is a consumer games publisher and, as of 2026-08-04, publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable API contract.
